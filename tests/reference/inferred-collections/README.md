# Inferred collection contracts

OWL rules require the complete [LIST antecedent](https://www.w3.org/TR/owl2-profiles/#Reasoning_in_OWL_2_RL_and_RDF_Graphs_using_Rules).
A partial prefix supplies no list operand. Complete literal cells retain their
RDF identity regardless of Python truth value. Every member-position pair is
checked, including the final pair and repeated terms in `AllDifferent`.

The 138 complete crates contain 41 explicit corrected-reference differences and
97 deeply unchanged controls. Productive branches and cycles complete normally,
including additional witness lengths through described `rdf:nil`. Inference
uses finite member/pair relations and property-chain product states rather than
enumerating potentially infinite sequences. Members from exclusive branches
are never paired unless they coexist on a complete witness. Resource budgets
remain finite; diagnostic rendering uses its separate report-graph traversal.

`capture.py` leaves installed Python untouched. Its corrected mode accepts only
the exact registered inputs, verifies original function hashes, and evaluates
independent backward fixed-point relations in `rules.py`. Key witnesses require
shared values at every property position; the separate
[key equality contracts](../key-equality/README.md) retain the original omission
and its independently corrected reports. All 138 collection reports stay unchanged.
Original and corrected captures retain all input bytes. Ground proofs cover
complete antecedents, member pairs,
8,192 branching graphs and 2,048 property-chain combinations.

`ordinary-reference.json` preserves fourteen historical ordinary
records, including exceptions and input hashes. Fresh original captures are
separate. Only the five named exception groups in `exception-order-proof.json`
permit their observed child permutations during capture verification: two
specified datatype groups with two children, two AllDifferent groups with
four, and the shared-prefix disjoint-property group with two. Every child and all other fields remain exact. This does not
change ordinary or live result comparison. The original upstream-defect suite
is unchanged.
